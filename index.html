Tap changer simulator

Put ratio and phase tap changers on any end of a two- or three-winding transformer, move the steps and see the load flow, the single-line diagram and which tap changer causes which part of the voltage change. Same model as the 64 CGMES 3.0 test cases (each tap changer is an ideal transformer at its terminal, v_terminal = t · v_internal); the solver reproduces all their SVs to 1e-11 pu. Tap changers per end

Each end (winding) can carry one ratio tap changer and one phase tap changer, as in CGMES. Move a step with its slider, or switch on automatic control: the tap changer then steps until its value is inside the band setpoint ± deadband/2 (deadband = full width, as CGMES targetDeadband), like a real controller.
