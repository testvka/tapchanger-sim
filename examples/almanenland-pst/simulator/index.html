PST Simulator · Almanenland

How phase-shifting transformers (PSTs) steer the flows in the imaginary 380 kV system Almanenland and its neighbours Westonia, Slavania and Sudlandia. Pick a scenario and an AC case, then move the steps of any of the 18 PSTs and watch the tie flows, the north–south flow and the line loadings. Every case in the case list is a full AC load flow of the GridLab model (2,742 nodes); a step you move yourself is estimated from AC sensitivities of the same model. Two-path model

The principle in one picture: a transfer T from north to south splits between the internal path A and a path B through a neighbour with the selected PST in it. Exact AC for two lossless paths at 380 kV: P = U·U'·sin(δ)/X. The PST adds its angle α (and its ratio) to path B only.
