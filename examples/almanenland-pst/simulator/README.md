# PST Simulator · Almanenland

Browser page (plain JavaScript, no build step, no server needed: open `index.html` directly) about the
phase-shifting transformers of Almanenland. It has the look and layout of the Tap Changer Simulator
(`cgmes30-tap-configurations/simulator`), but works on the whole Almanenland grid:

- **Scenario and AC case:** the six scenarios of [`../PST_SCENARIOS.md`](../PST_SCENARIOS.md), each with its AC load flows
  (as operated, every PST on neutral, Slavania blocks, tie outage, large-angle sweeps, ...).
- **Map:** the 380 kV grid of the four countries, line loading or the change against "as operated", tie flow
  arrows, the north → south cut and the 18 PSTs (hover for numbers, click a PST to select it).
- **Phase shifter:** move the step of any PST. A step that matches an AC case shows that case ("AC load flow").
  Any other step is estimated from the AC sensitivities (±3 steps) of the scenario ("estimate").
- **Phasor:** U in, U out and the booster voltage of the selected PST at its step, with every step of its real tap
  table (symmetrical, asymmetrical 70° / 90°, tabular).
- **Two-path model:** exact AC split of a transfer between an internal path and a path with the PST.
- **Sweep (S1):** the border flows while Slavania's six PSTs step from neutral to their end stops (9 AC load flows),
  with a table view.

Files: `index.html` (layout and styles), `pst.js` (the page), `pst_data.js` (generated by
`../tools/build_page_data.py` from `../results/`). Light and dark mode follow the system setting.
